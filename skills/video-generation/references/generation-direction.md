# Direct generated images and video shots

Use for AI image/video B-roll, keyframes or continuity studies. This is a directing workflow across providers, not a list of speculative model IDs. Inspect the currently selected provider's schema, reference-image support, camera controls, duration, resolution and result terms before execution.

## Route by the needed material and actual capability

Use authentic captures/vendor assets for exact product evidence, local code/vector rendering for exact type/data, a host-native image tool for generated stills when available, and an actual video tool/provider for generated takes. Stage returned local media and retain the generating tool/prompt/provenance. Host-native media tools and the toolkit's fal queue adapter are distinct execution paths; don't invent a dependency on fal credentials when the host already provides the required tool.

When no suitable generator exists, finish the look/shot specification and a truthful handoff or use explicitly chosen local 3D/illustrative motion. Do not silently replace requested live-action generation with an unlabeled slideshow. Check current capability, terms and cost at the chosen execution boundary.

## Shot prompt as a production note

Write subject/identity, one observable action, environment, framing/lens intention, camera start→end, light/material, duration, aspect ratio, continuity anchors and exclusions. Specify what must remain unchanged. A useful brief is “three-quarter close-up of the supplied matte device; camera trucks past a near foreground edge to reveal its connector; soft side key, dark neutral set; device stays still; no invented lettering.” It is more controllable than “ultra cinematic amazing dynamic 8K.”

Separate subject motion from camera motion. Use one principal move per short take. Establish where it starts, what it reveals and how it lands. Contradictory instructions such as static camera plus orbit plus drone dive often produce incoherent motion. If a timed exact state, layout or data trace matters, render it deterministically instead.

## Continuity and shot coverage

Anchor identity, silhouette, palette, wardrobe/material, light direction, set geography, lens language and screen direction. Reuse authorized references and stable inputs when the provider supports them. Design coverage: an establishing/context view, useful detail/action and consequence. Each take should have a role in the edit rather than being another dramatic generic fly-through.

A start/end image does not guarantee that the intervening motion obeys object permanence. Review occlusion, hands, wheels, reflections, facial stability, geometry, texture swimming and letters through the whole shot. Check the first and last frames as well as a montage of middle frames. Avoid hiding broken motion under a speed ramp.

## Stage experiments economically

Validate a frame or short low-cost animatic first when useful. Select one visual direction; generate a bounded coverage set with the user's configured budget, then inspect before requesting alternatives. State why a take failed—subject changes shape at 1.8s, camera crosses the action axis, landing too tight—so the next prompt changes the relevant variable. More random rerolls are not creative direction.

Keep a shot log with prompt, seed/reference inputs where available, provider/model/endpoint revision, request ID, cost reservation, result path, license and review. A failed/ambiguous paid request may still be running; inspect its existing status rather than retrying blindly. Cloud adapters remain opt-in, credentialed and budgeted. Do not upload private references to an unselected provider.

## Conform the chosen take

Match output FPS/timing, crop, color and grain to the neighboring shots. Preserve usable handles. Put exact typography/brand assets/captions in the editable compositor rather than relying on generated glyphs. Mix sound deliberately; a silent video provider does not supply implied real-world audio. Deliver generation inputs and provenance with the chosen footage.

Live generation quality is unverified until a real configured job is executed and reviewed. The toolkit's mocked transport tests verify request/budget/result handling, not cinematography or consistency. For local procedural 3D, use [cinematography](../../video-motion/references/cinematography.md) when it fits the visual idea.
