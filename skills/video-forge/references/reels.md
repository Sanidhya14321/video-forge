# Short-form choreography

Choose rhythm for the use case. Combine dense kinetic editing, continuous spatial traversal and aggressive montage when the story benefits. The plugin does not impose one cut interval or a named preset sequence.

Author a score in frames: shots, reading windows, transitions, internal accents and sound. Starting points for an explicitly high-energy brief are 0.25–0.9-second visual phrases, 2–8-frame accents and occasional 1–3-frame fragments inside a burst. These are adjustable creative choices, not platform rules or engagement predictions. An essential thought or proof can remain continuously readable while the surrounding framing, background or layers change.

Build a focal path: opening tension/result → transformation → evidence → payoff. Link adjacent shots by a subject, letterform, mask edge, direction or velocity. Vary scale, density and movement. Use anticipation/travel/settle selectively; a hard cut need not have an entrance tween. Keep a stable reading layer through some rapid edits. A few longer reading moments can make the dense sections feel faster.

Choose actual assets and define a visual thesis before selecting effects. Read [code-motion](../../video-motion/references/code-motion.md) for original composable mechanisms. Use the [version-2 code project](../../../docs/code-projects.md), not a fixed sequence of heading panels. Original procedural graphics and footage can share a scene; the video has no need for hover/scroll input.

Frame all artwork full bleed where useful. Protect important copy, faces, captions and genuine UI individually using the intended platform overlay. Do not shrink the whole film into the old conservative content band. Compose separately for different aspect ratios. Avoid repeated full-screen flashes as a substitute for fast editing.

Sound carries the edit: place transients on selected changes, pre-lap the next shot where useful, leave some beats unaccented, and keep voice intelligible. Preserve measured caption timing through actual cuts. A musical beat grid is a tool, not the complete story score.

Watch the encoded moving result with audio and sound off, at phone size and native resolution. Inspect fastest frames, motion continuity, proof readability, empty transitions, blur, source crop quality and the final frame/loop. Metadata warnings are prompts for inspection; they do not establish taste, retention or platform performance. Platform constraints are maintained separately in [platforms](platforms.md).

Legacy version-1 reel/pop examples remain available for structured tasks. They are not the default creative grammar for a new sophisticated brief.
