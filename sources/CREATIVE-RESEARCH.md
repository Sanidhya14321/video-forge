# Creative research and implementation — 20 October 2026

The previous output was technically valid but relied too heavily on a narrow overlay vocabulary. This update separates creative direction, asset casting, cinematography, editing and execution. It preserves the original 100-entry register and adds a fresh, pinned mechanism-level audit.

## Decisions after research

- Put a visual argument and asset choice before a template. A recipe is a mechanism to restage, not a universal story.
- Stage real 3D camera travel with a target, depth cues and a useful landing; do not call a 2D scale/tilt a 3D traversal.
- Distinguish live render loops from frame capture: ordinary R3F advice can conflict with Remotion determinism.
- Do not inherit an upstream project's global slow-pacing defaults or effect quotas. Tune rhythm to speech, reading load, assets, destination and the user's intent.
- Use Premiere for editorial/finishing concepts and a concrete handoff. Adobe's UXP samples and the community CEP bridge are different integration paths; neither is implemented merely by documenting it.
- Keep asset rights separate from code licenses. Shotcraft audio includes separate/uncertain provenance; LosslessCut's GPL implementation is not copied. The bundled procedural recipe kit is original MIT material.

## Concrete deliverables and limits

The skills now route to original taste/critique, asset casting, generation continuity, editing grammar, Premiere, platform and cinematography references. Twelve authored recipes can be loaded individually. Native tools add source scouting and true procedural 3D worlds with camera/object keys and local image planes. Tests and rendered evidence are recorded in the release validation, separately from subjective taste.

No model weights were fine-tuned, live Adobe application controlled, cloud generation purchased, audience benchmark measured, or complete upstream application recreated. Structural skill checks do not establish that Claude/Codex will always make strong creative decisions. See the [behavioral evaluation cases](https://github.com/Sanidhya14321/video-forge/blob/v0.7.0/docs/creative-evaluation.md) for future host trials.
