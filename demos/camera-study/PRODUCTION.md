# Camera and asset study

Original 12-second, 360-frame, 1080×1920 native Three.js/Remotion validation film. Rebuild `examples/camera-traversal.json`; its assets are original MIT procedural geometry, a self-contained rounded speaker GLB, a signal image plane, and a 120 BPM score/SFX.

Three shot functions: travel through foreground depth to a destination; separate/reassemble meaningful layers with a camera arc; move from product detail to a stable readable hero. This is a foundation/asset study, not a real product claim, photoreal AI generation, independent agent trial, or engagement benchmark.

Visual review corrected hero scale, a tight assembly crop and a distracting edge texture. The expanded preview includes camera-key/midpoint frames. Final encoded samples, decoding, stream/frame measurements and loudness are in validation.json; out-of-order determinism evidence is recorded separately.
