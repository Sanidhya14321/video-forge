# Video Forge: Creative Arcade

30 seconds, 1080×1920, 30fps. Explain the same project with a contrasting visual language. Full-bleed flat cobalt, coral, pink, lime, yellow and cream; no CSS or SVG gradients. Original vector graphics; no stock or paid generation.

Story: a small brief is fed into a playful creative machine, connected to Claude/Codex, and transformed into scenes, code, data, cuts, voice, words and multiple formats. A mischievous play-button character links the shots.

22 shots on a 160 BPM grid with deliberately varied durations; exactly 900 frames using cumulative frame boundaries. The hook appears immediately. Commands/data remain literal and receive a readable hold. Key copy and captions use Video Forge's protected layout band; decorative motion fills the rest of the frame.

Effects: elastic squash/stretch, flat extruded lettering, sticker outlines, offset color echoes, halftone dots, checkerboard ribbons, speed lines, confetti, spark particles, shape morphs, film cuts, spinning gears, camera bumps, masked reveals, wipes, boombox cones, waveform bars, word stamps, and matchable play-object transitions. No full-screen flash spam; the focal action stays clear.

Original local AI narration and original stereo chiptune/funk score with designed pops, boings, clicks and zaps. Actual faster-whisper timing supplies captions. Native review/preview/run/inspect produce the complete editable job. Comparison preserves the existing minimal reel, supplies synchronized playback and a labelled side-by-side export.
