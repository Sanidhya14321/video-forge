## Production improvements

- Six native, frame-driven overlay presets with editable timing, position, brand colors and local assets.
- Screenshot focus/crop and pointer coordinates use shared geometry. Captions stay above full-screen overlays.
- Prepared footage now supports entry/peak/exit preview frames; staged assets are deduplicated and renderer snapshots preserve the new modules.
- Conditional brand-edit guidance covers audience, claims and evidence, genuine product visuals, face/caption placement, mobile reading time, audio, asset credits and privacy.
- Reproducible comparison ZIP metadata completes deterministic packaging.
